Why multiple layers?

Depth lets a network build complex ideas from simpler ones. Each layer can recombine patterns detected by earlier layers.

01

Early layers detect parts

They respond to small, local patterns in the raw input.

image example → edges, colors
02

Middle layers combine parts

They assemble those patterns into more useful structures.

edges → corners, textures
03

Later layers support a decision

They represent task-specific concepts used by the output.

shapes → object category

The six ideas to remember

InputsNumbers that represent the example being processed.
02
WeightsLearned multipliers that control a connection’s influence.
03
BiasA learned offset that shifts when a neuron activates.
04
NodesSmall computation units: combine, shift, activate.
05
LayersStages that build representations from simple to complex.
